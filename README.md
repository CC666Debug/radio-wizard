# RadioWizard

Find any internet radio station, then play it on your phone or cast it to Nest / Google Cast speakers.

One search goes to ten free radio directories at once and merges the results:
Radio Browser, TuneIn, SHOUTcast, Radio Garden, radio.net, iHeartRadio, laut.fm, Icecast, OnlineRadioBox and SomaFM.
The same station found in several places becomes one entry with backup links.

- **Website:** https://cc666debug.github.io/radio-wizard/ (Radio Browser, iHeartRadio, laut.fm, Icecast and SomaFM; the others block websites)
- **Android app:** searches all ten and tests every station before listing it. Download: https://github.com/CC666Debug/radio-wizard-android/releases/latest/download/RadioWizard.apk

Favorites and recently played stay on your device. Visits are counted with GoatCounter (no cookies).
Stations belong to their owners; RadioWizard only links to their public streams.
